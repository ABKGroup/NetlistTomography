# Sensitivity of TomoGNN to Netlist-Mapping Noise

This study quantifies how much noise the placeOpt→synthesis netlist mapping
(Algorithm 1 in the paper) introduces, and how sensitive TomoGNN is to this
noise.

We perform an injected-noise sensitivity study. For 2%, 5%, 10%, and 20% of
instances that are randomly selected, we perturb their mapped features and
apply the trained TomoGNN to the perturbed inputs without retraining, then
re-cluster and regenerate the soft guides. For each selected instance, we
replace its location $(x, y)$ with $(x+\delta_x, y+\delta_y)$, where
$\delta_x$ and $\delta_y$ are randomly sampled from
$(-r_{adapt}, r_{adapt})$; and we perturb the slack of its connected nets
$s \to s+\delta s$, where $\delta s$ is randomly sampled from
$[-0.05T_{\mathrm{clk}}, 0.05T_{\mathrm{clk}}]$.

We use normalized mutual information (NMI) to measure cluster stability between
the clean-mapping result and the noisy-mapping result generated by TomoGNN. NMI
ranges from 0 to 1, where 1 means identical clustering and higher values
indicate more stable soft-guide generation under injected mapping noise.

Experimental results are shown below. Across various designs in NG45 and GF12,
NMI remains above 0.91 even when 20% of mapped instance features are perturbed.
These results demonstrate that TomoGNN is robust to mapping noise. This
robustness comes from multiple tomography views, quality-aware fusion, and
HDBSCAN soft clustering, which can leave uncertain instances unclustered rather
than forcing them into hard clusters.

The underlying numbers are provided in
[`data/noise_summary.csv`](data/noise_summary.csv).

| BP_Quad_NG45 | 0.952 | 0.941 | 0.929 | 0.913 |
| SweRV_GF12   | 0.978 | 0.967 | 0.959 | 0.945 |
| Ariane_GF12  | 0.976 | 0.967 | 0.955 | 0.944 |
